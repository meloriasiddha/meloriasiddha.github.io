About us

Meloria – Siddha & Varma Clinic (legal name: Meloria Siddha and Varma Clinic) is a Siddha medicine and Varmam therapy clinic in Maharaja Nagar, Tirunelveli, run by Dr J J Amala Janice Seelan, BSMS, Siddha physician, specialist in diabetes and Varmam, practising since November 2023.

What we do

  • Siddha consultations at the clinic and online
  • Lifestyle programs for diabetes, PCOS, weight loss, fatty liver and cholesterol, with diet plans and follow-up in our app
  • Varmam therapy, Varma Thokkanam, Mooligai Otradam and yoga for pain
  • Siddha medicines prepared and packed by the clinic
  • For patients from outside Tirunelveli: online pain consultation and stay-and-treat admission at our partner hospital, Cure Siddha Hospital

Our app

Patients use the Meloria Siddha app to book visits, pay fees, post readings and diet, chat with the clinic, see reports and order medicines.
