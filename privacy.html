Privacy policy

This policy explains how Meloria Siddha and Varma Clinic ("the clinic", "we") handles personal and health information collected through the Meloria Siddha patient app and at the clinic. What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, date of birth, and a PIN you set.
  • Health information you share: readings such as blood sugar, weight, blood pressure, temperature; diet and exercise posts; symptoms; messages to the clinic; photos and reports you upload.
  • Clinic records: visits, prescriptions, lab and scan results, treatment sessions, programs and bills.
  • Bookings and orders: appointments, medicine orders and delivery address.
  • Payments: handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and reference, not your full card or bank details.
  • Device data: a notification token so we can send reminders and messages.

Why we use it

  • To provide your consultation, treatment and follow-up.
  • To show your records, charts and reports to you in the app.
  • To send reminders (appointments, readings, medicines, program renewals) and replies from the clinic.
  • For billing and to keep medical records as required by law.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Consent

When you first use the app you are asked to agree to this use of your health information. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account (see below).

Who can see it

Only the clinic's doctor and authorised staff. Your information is stored and processed by these service providers on our behalf:

  • Supabase – secure cloud database and file storage (encrypted in transit and at rest).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivering notifications. Notification text does not include your readings or results.
  • Razorpay – online payments.

We may share information with another doctor or hospital only for your care (for example, a referral letter) or when required by law.

How long we keep it

Treatment records are kept permanently. Your clinic records (visits, prescriptions, test and scan results, readings, treatment sessions and bills) are medical records. The clinic keeps them for the life of the clinic and does not delete them, so that your full history is available for your care and as required by law (at least 3 years under Indian medical record rules). You can ask for a copy at any time.

Information from app sign-ups that never became clinic patients is deleted when the account is deleted. Notification tokens are deleted when you delete your account or uninstall the app.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or update your information, withdraw consent, or delete your app account. Contact us at meloriasiddha@gmail.com or +91 93446 98304. We respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. See how to delete your account.

Children

Patients under 18 should use the app with a parent or guardian, who gives consent on their behalf.

Security

Access is protected by login, the clinic app is locked with fingerprint or PIN, and data is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your PIN private.

Changes

We will post any changes here and, if they are important, tell you in the app.
