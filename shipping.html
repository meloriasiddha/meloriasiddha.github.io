Shipping and delivery

We send prescribed Siddha medicines to our patients. Medicines are supplied only on the clinic doctor's prescription.

Pickup in Tirunelveli

Patients in Tirunelveli can choose free pickup at the clinic (Monday – Saturday, 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM). We tell you in the app when the order is ready.

Courier delivery

  • We deliver within India through courier services.
  • Charges depend on the delivery distance and the weight of the parcel. The exact charge is shown to you before you pay.
  • Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 working days after payment.
  • Delivery usually takes 2–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location and the courier service.
  • We share the courier name and tracking number once dispatched.

Problems with delivery

If your parcel is delayed, lost, wrong or damaged, contact us on +91 93446 98304 or meloriasiddha@gmail.com. Wrong or damaged items reported within 48 hours are replaced or refunded – see Cancellation and refunds.
