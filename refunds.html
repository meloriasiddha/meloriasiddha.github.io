Cancellation and refunds

Consultations and therapy appointments

  • Cancelled 24 hours or more before the appointment: full refund.
  • Cancelled less than 24 hours before, or missed: no refund, but you may reschedule once free of charge.
  • Cancelled by the clinic (or the slot could not be confirmed after payment): full refund, or a new time if you prefer.

Programs

  • Program fees are not refundable once the program has started.
  • If you cannot continue for a while (illness, travel), your remaining months can be rescheduled – paused and continued later – by arrangement with the doctor.
  • If you cancel before the program starts, you get a full refund.

Medicine orders

  • Cancelled before dispatch: full refund (including any courier charge paid).
  • After dispatch, orders cannot be cancelled. Medicines cannot be returned once delivered, for safety reasons.
  • Wrong or damaged items: tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We replace the item or refund it.

How refunds are paid

  • Request a cancellation in the app, by phone/WhatsApp on +91 93446 98304, or by email to meloriasiddha@gmail.com.
  • Approved refunds are started within 2 working days and reach your original payment method (UPI, card, net banking) within 5–7 working days.
  • Payments made in cash at the clinic are refunded in cash or by UPI.
