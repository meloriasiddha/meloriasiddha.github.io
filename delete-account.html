Delete your Meloria Siddha app account

This page is for the Meloria Siddha patient app by Meloria Siddha and Varma Clinic.

In the app

  1. Open the Meloria Siddha app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email meloriasiddha@gmail.com or call +91 93446 98304 from your registered phone number and ask us to delete your app account. We do it within 7 days and confirm to you.

What is deleted

  • Your app login, phone number and PIN
  • Your notification settings and device tokens
  • If you never visited the clinic: your sign-up details are deleted completely

What is kept

If you have been treated at the clinic, your treatment records (visits, prescriptions, test results, readings and bills) are kept permanently as medical records and are not deleted. After you delete your account they are no longer linked to an app login. You may ask for a copy at any time.
